Top 3 best places to see in Cracow

Wawel Royal Castle

This magnificent royal residence offers an unforgettable journey through centuries of Polish kings, breathtaking Renaissance architecture and national legend.

Main Market Square

Step into the vibrant heart of Cracow's Old Town at Europe's largest medieval square, where iconic landmarks like the Renaissance Cloth Hall and the towering spires of St. Mary's Basilica await.

Kościuszko's Mound

Surrounded by 19th-century fortress walls, this earthwork monument offers the perfect mix of rich history, scenic walking paths and picturesque, panoramic views across the entire city.
